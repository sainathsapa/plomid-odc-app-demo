# Re-enactment shot list (for the owner — phone/laptop camera, parked car or chair mock)

Consent: self-recorded, single actor. 10–20 s per shot, daylight + one night
shot with cabin light. Upload originals to footage/ (gitignored).

1. `REEN_empty_crs.mp4` — empty child/baby seat (or laundry basket as stand-in)
   alone in frame, 3 angles. Fills the empty-CRS gap.
2. `REEN_blanket.mp4` — chair with blanket draped over (empty), then hand
   waving underneath (motion-without-visibility). Occlusion-cause evidence.
3. `REEN_belt_misuse.mp4` — seatbelt routed behind back + buckled; then
   correctly worn. Misuse pair for belt geometry.
4. `REEN_night_occupant.mp4` — occupant in dark cabin with dome light only.
   Night recall evidence.
5. `REEN_entry_exit.mp4` — actor sits, stays 10 s, leaves; door open/close
   audible. Temporal entry/exit ground truth.
6. `REEN_two_occupants.mp4` — two people, one partially occluded throughout.
   Association-under-occlusion evidence.

Name files exactly as above; the validation lab picks them up by name.
